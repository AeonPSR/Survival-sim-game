# Scenario Design – Attribute Reference

A scenario is rolled from 5 elements. Certain combinations are intentionally incompatible — finding the right approach to the right menace is a core part of the game.

---

## Setting

Base settings (pick one):
- Steampunk
- Modern
- Sci-fi
- Medieval

Modifiers (can be stacked on top of a base setting, combinable with each other):
- Wasteland *(always a wasteland of a prior civilization)*
- Fantasy

---

## The Menace

- Extreme Cold
- Extreme Heat
- Resource Scarcity
- Zombies
- Social Conflict
- Nuclear Fallout
- Pandemic
- Planetary Annihilation *(only compatible with settings that can support it, e.g. Sci-fi, Fantasy)*
- Totalitarianism

---

## The Group

- Society
- Large
- Medium
- Small
- Solo

---

## The Mac Guffin

- Central Infrastructure
- Train

---

## The Objective

- Survive *(reserved for scenarios where pure survival is genuinely the primary goal)*
- Adapt *(new normal)*
- Beat the Menace
- Find a Location
- Understand Something


Potential future idea
- Contain

---

## Design Notes

- Modifiers cannot exist alone — they always modify a base Setting.
- Some attribute combinations are intentionally mutually exclusive. The constraint matrix is a feature.
- "Survive" should only appear when survival itself is the core point, not as a default fallback.
